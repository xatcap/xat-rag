Claims handling

A claim for lost goods opens when the tracking record has shown no movement for nine working days. The claims officer attaches the commercial invoice and the signed proof of collection, then sends the file to the carrier through its online claims desk. Each file receives a reference which starts with the letters HCL.

Compensation for road transport is limited by convention to 8.33 special drawing rights per kilogram of gross weight. Customers who need more cover may buy the declared value option when they book. Consequential loss such as a missed sale is excluded from the standard terms and from most of the optional cover.

Claims above 5000 euros go to the regional claims panel, which meets on alternate Thursdays. The panel reviews photographs, driver statements and any survey report before it agrees a settlement. Its decision is recorded in the claims register and copied to the account manager of the customer concerned.

Hazardous goods

Lithium batteries shipped by road travel under UN 3480 unless they are packed with equipment. Packages carry the battery mark and a telephone number for further information. The dangerous goods adviser audits a sample of shipments and reports any faults to the operations director in an annual written report.

Drivers carrying dangerous goods above the exempt threshold must hold a valid ADR certificate and must complete a refresher every five years. Vehicles are fitted with orange plates and two fire extinguishers, and the transport document stays in the cab within reach of the driver for the entire journey.

Aerosols are stored in the caged area at the east wall, separated from oxidising substances by at least three metres. Stock in the cage is capped at 600 kilograms of net contents in order to stay within the conditions of the site permit. Damaged cans go into the punctured aerosol drum and are removed by a licensed contractor.
